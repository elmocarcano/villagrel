What’s on around Lake Como this week

Looking for things to do on Lake Como this week? Here is our hand-picked guide to the events worth your evening — concerts, festivals, open-air cinema, football and more — all within about thirty minutes of Villa Grel. Written by us, your hosts, and updated every week.

This week the lake goes football-mad: the international Como Cup takes over the lakeside Sinigaglia stadium from Tuesday to Saturday, with Como 1907, Crystal Palace, RC Lens, FC Famalicão, AlUla SC and Villarreal — fifteen minutes from the villa.

Sunday 26 July

Cernobbio & Como
🎤 Music · Festival

Lake Sound Park — closing night

📍 Villa Erba, Cernobbio · from 19:00 · ~15 min by car

The final night of Cernobbio’s month-long lakeside music festival, in the grand grounds of Villa Erba. Even if you don’t catch the headline act, the market, food and setting by the water make for a memorable evening. Check the official page for tonight’s line-up.

💃 Dance

Ballando tra le Stelle — dancing under the stars

📍 Piazza Martinelli, Como · evening · ~15 min by car

Part of the city’s “Como… che spettacolo!” summer programme: a square turned open-air dance floor with live music. Free, easy and pure Italian summer — wander in and watch, or join in.

Summer art in the city

📍 Central Como · through late September · ~15 min by car
